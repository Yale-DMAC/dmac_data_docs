# Requesting ArchivesSpace Support From SCMS 

## Overview 

Our unit provides support for ArchivesSpace administration, troubleshooting, training, documentation, and system enhancements. 

**Examples include:**
- Reporting system issues or bugs 
- Requesting user accounts, password resets, or permission changes 
- Granting access to repositories 
- Training and documentation requests 
- Troubleshooting ArchivesSpace functionality 
- Changes to Archives at Yale 
- Feature requests and enhancement suggestions 
- Policy and workflow questions related to ArchivesSpace 

To ensure requests are completed as efficiently as possible, please provide as much detail as you can when submitting a request. 

Incomplete or unclear requests may require follow-up questions and can delay resolution. 

## Before Submitting a Request 

Consider the following questions: 
- What type of support do you need? 
- Which repository or repositories are involved? 
- What were you trying to accomplish? 
- What happened? 
- What did you expect to happen instead? 
- Is the issue affecting a single record, multiple records, or all users? 
- Can the issue be reproduced? 
- If requesting permissions or access, what level of access is needed? 
- Do you have screenshots, error messages, record IDs, or other examples? Providing examples is often the fastest way to help us understand and resolve your request. 

## Helpful Examples 

**Instead of:**
>I can't edit records. 

**Consider:**
>I am unable to edit Resource records in the MSSA Repository. When I attempt to save changes, the Save button is disabled. This issue began on June 10 and occurs on multiple records. My username is js345. Other staff in the repository appear to be unaffected. Screenshots are attached. 

**Instead of:**
> I need permissions added to Jane Smith in ArchivesSpace. 

**Consider:**
>I need permissions added for Jane Smith in the BRBL repository and the YCBA repository. Their netid is js678, and they require archivist level permissions in both production and test. The user should have permissions equivalent to the Archivist role currently assigned to John Doe whose netid is jd344. 

## What Happens Next 

After reviewing your request, we may: 
- Clarify requirements or gather additional information 
- Review screenshots, error messages, and examples 
- Troubleshoot and reproduce reported issues 
- Coordinate with service owners, advisory groups, or external developers when needed 
- Recommend solutions, training, documentation, or workflow changes
