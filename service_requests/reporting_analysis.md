# Requesting Reports from SCMS 

## Overview 

Our unit creates custom reports using data from ArchivesSpace and Alma. To ensure reports are accurate and delivered as efficiently as possible, please provide as much detail as you can when submitting a request. 

Incomplete or vague requests may require follow-up questions and can delay delivery. 

## Before Submitting a Request 

Consider the following questions: 
- Who will use the report? 
- What records should be included? 
- What records should be excluded? 
- What information needs to appear in the report? 
- How often will the report be needed? 
- How many records are estimated to be included in the report? 
- Do you already have an example of the data you are looking for? Providing examples is often the fastest way to help us understand your request. 

## Helpful Examples 

**Instead of:**
> I need a report of recently updated collections. 

**Consider:**
>I need a report of collection-level resource records in the BRBL repository updated between January 1 and March 31, including title, identifier, access restriction notes, and last modified date. The report should exclude suppressed records and records without active access restriction notes. 
>Examples of records that should appear: 
>- Resource ID: 13456 
>-  MMS ID: 99123456983948 

## What Happens Next 

After reviewing your request, we may: 
- Clarify requirements 
- Suggest modifications to improve accuracy or performance
