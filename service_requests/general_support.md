# Requesting General Support From SCMS 

## Overview 

General Support is intended for requests that do not clearly fit into one of our other service categories or when you are unsure about which type of service you need. 

**Examples include:**
- General Metadata Services staff training 
- Offering advice or consultations on projects 
- Questions about available services 
- Requests that may involve multiple service areas 
- Requests where the desired outcome is known, but the solution is not 
- General questions related to library systems, data, workflows, or tools 

If you are unsure whether your request is related to reporting, data transformation, data remediation, data creation or enhancement, tool or workflow support, or ArchivesSpace support, select General Support and we will help determine the appropriate next steps. 

## Before Submitting a Request 

Consider the following questions: 
- What are you trying to accomplish? 
- What problem are you trying to solve? 
- Which systems, tools, or workflows are involved? 
- What outcome are you hoping to achieve? 
- Do you have examples, screenshots, documentation, or sample records that may help explain the request? Providing examples is often the fastest way to help us understand your request. 

## Helpful Examples 

**Instead of:**
>I need help with some ArchivesSpace data. 

**Consider:**
>We are reviewing collection records in ArchivesSpace and have identified several issues, but we are not sure whether they require reporting, remediation, or a workflow change. Examples of the affected records are attached, along with a description of the issues we have identified. 

**Instead of:**
>Can someone help with this project? 

**Consider:**
>We are planning a project involving data currently stored in spreadsheets and ArchivesSpace. We are still defining requirements and would like guidance on possible approaches, system capabilities, and next steps before submitting a more specific request. 

## What Happens Next 

After reviewing your request, we may: 
- Clarify requirements and goals 
- Ask follow-up questions 
- Recommend a more specific service category 
- Identify possible approaches or solutions 
- Connect you with the appropriate staff or resources
