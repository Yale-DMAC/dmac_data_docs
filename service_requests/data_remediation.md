# Requesting Data Remediation from SCMS 
## Overview 
Our unit provides data remediation services for projects involving bulk corrections, updates, and removal of existing data. 

**Examples include:**
- Normalizing subject heading variants 
- Removing Item records from Holdings records 
- Updating preferred citation notes with revised contact information 
- Correcting outdated values across large numbers of records 
- Removing obsolete or incorrect data from records 

To ensure remediation projects are completed as efficiently and accurately as possible, please provide as much detail as you can when submitting a request. 

Incomplete or unclear requests may require follow-up questions and can delay project timelines. 

## Before Submitting a Request 

Consider the following questions: 
- What data needs to be corrected, updated, or removed? 
- How can the affected records be identified? 
- How many records are estimated to be affected? 
- What should the corrected data look like? 
- Are there records that should be excluded from the remediation? 
- How will the success of the remediation be verified?  
- Are there examples of records that should and should not be modified? Providing examples is often the fastest way to help us understand your request. 

## Helpful Examples 

**Instead of:**  
> We have inconsistent subject headings. 

**Consider:**  
> Subject headings using "Civil War--United States" should be updated to "United States--History--Civil War, 1861-1865". Records already using the preferred heading should not be modified. 

## What Happens Next 

After reviewing your request, we may: 
- Clarify remediation requirements 
- Review sample records 
- Identify affected records 
- Confirm remediation rules and exceptions
